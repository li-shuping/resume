Shuping Li

Ph.D. Candidate | Functional Genomics | Immunology | Single-Cell & Computational Genomics

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Physiology at the University of Oklahoma Health Campus, with research experience in functional genomics, immunology, single-cell and bulk transcriptomics, and immune-mediated disease. My work focuses on translating disease-associated genetic variation into experimentally testable regulatory mechanisms.

Research Profile

My current dissertation research investigates causal variant discovery and functional characterization in the systemic lupus erythematosus-associated STAT4 locus. I integrate pooled CRISPR perturbation, HCR-FlowFISH, CRISPR base editing, ATAC-seq, transcriptomics, and molecular validation to study regulatory mechanisms controlling immune gene expression.
