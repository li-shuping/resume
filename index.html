Shuping Li

Ph.D. Candidate | Functional Genomics | Immunology | Single-Cell & Computational Genomics

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Physiology at the University of Oklahoma Health Campus, with research experience in functional genomics, immunology, single-cell and bulk transcriptomics, and immune-mediated disease. My work focuses on translating disease-associated genetic variation into experimentally testable regulatory mechanisms.

Education

  • Ph.D. Candidate, Physiology, University of Oklahoma Health Campus, 2021-Present
  • Master's Degree, Internal Medicine, Sun Yat-sen University, 2018-2021
  • Bachelor's Degree, Clinical Medicine, Gannan Medical University, 2013-2018

Research Profile

My current dissertation research investigates causal variant discovery and functional characterization in the systemic lupus erythematosus-associated STAT4 locus. I integrate pooled CRISPR perturbation, HCR-FlowFISH, CRISPR base editing, ATAC-seq, transcriptomics, and molecular validation to study regulatory mechanisms controlling immune gene expression.

Research Interests

  • Functional genomics of immune-mediated disease
  • Systemic lupus erythematosus and autoimmune disease genetics
  • Single-cell and bulk transcriptomic analysis
  • Pooled CRISPRi screening and CRISPR base editing
  • Regulatory variant discovery and characterization
  • Multi-omics integration and computational biology

Technical Expertise

  • Functional genomics: CRISPRi screening, sgRNA library design, HCR-FlowFISH, CRISPR base editing
  • Genomics: ATAC-seq, bulk RNA-seq, single-cell RNA-seq, Cell Ranger, Seurat, Harmony
  • Computational analysis: R, Python, Linux, HPC, WGCNA, pathway analysis, data visualization
  • Molecular biology: PCR/qPCR, Western blot, ELISA, immunofluorescence, flow cytometry
  • Cell and animal models: mammalian cell culture, 3D oncosphere assays, mouse breeding and genotyping
